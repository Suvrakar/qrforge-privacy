QRForge: QR & Barcode Scanner — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

QRForge ("the app") scans and creates QR codes and barcodes. The app does not collect, upload or sell your personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising SDK and no servers of its own. Everything you scan or create stays on your phone.

What stays on your device

  • Scan and creation history (the text of codes you scanned or created, the format and the time) is stored in a private database on your phone. Delete items from the History screen, or clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, to remove it.
  • Camera frames are analysed on the device to find codes and are never stored or sent anywhere. The photo shown on the result page is kept in memory only, and is saved only if you tap Save.
  • Images you pick (a gallery photo to scan, or a logo for a code) are read on the device only.

Permissions

Purchases

Payments for QRForge Pro are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. The app only learns from Google Play whether you own Pro. Google's handling of that data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Things you should know

  • Links, Wi-Fi, contacts and other actions. When you tap an action such as "Open link", your phone's browser or another app opens it. That app's own privacy policy then applies. Check where a link goes before opening it.
  • Sharing. If you share a code or image, the content goes to the app you choose.
  • Expiring codes. The expiry time is written inside the QR code itself. Only QRForge enforces it. Other scanners ignore it, and anyone can edit a code, so it is a convenience and not a security feature.
  • Location codes contain the coordinates in plain form. Anyone who scans or receives the code can see them. Only share them with people you trust.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Data we collect and share

None. We have no servers that receive your data, so there is nothing to share, sell or delete on our side.
